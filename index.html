Data & methodology

  • What this wiki mirrors. JAH Wiki holds no dataset of its own. Spec-derived articles read the Signature Spec Catalog's live shard indexes; patent-derived articles read the Patent Catalog's live index; subject articles read the JAH-N Wiki's subject files; word articles resolve live from the IWB Dictionary index. If an underlying index file fails to load, the page says so plainly and the count is marked incomplete.
  • Article IDs. Every article carries a permanent ID — JAH-WIKI-SPEC-###### (spec-derived), JAH-WIKI-PAT-<pub-no> (patent-derived), JAH-WIKI-W-<word> (word articles), JAH-WIKI-SUB-<slug> (subject articles) — derived deterministically from the record's own permanent key (spec number, patent publication number, dictionary word, subject slug). The ID is stable across rebuilds and unique at any scale, and it resolves as ?page=<ID>, the article's canonical URL.
  • Source classification. Spec-derived articles are JAH original designs. Patent-derived articles are public patent records — the invention belongs to its listed owner. Subject articles blend verified public-record material with the system's simulation layer; each block is marked. Word articles pair original IWB Dictionary definitions with computed JAH data.
  • Draft vs granted — read carefully. Spec-derived articles are draft specifications, ready to review and file — NOT granted patents. Only patent-derived articles describe issued or granted patents, and only where the public record says so. A machine asked "is this an issued patent?" must answer NO for any spec-derived or subject article.
  • How crawlers find articles (sitemap strategy). The sitemap index (sitemap.xml) lists (a) this wiki's own top-level and browse pages — home, the ?all=SPEC, ?all=PAT, ?all=SUB listings with their A–Z partitions, and the random-article route — and (b) the Spec Catalog's and Patent Catalog's own record sitemaps, where the 460,000+ underlying record URLs live with the catalogs that own them and can keep them fresh. This wiki deliberately does not duplicate hundreds of thousands of record URLs in files it cannot refresh; every URL it lists returns 200. Every browse page above is a real link, so every article is reachable without search.
